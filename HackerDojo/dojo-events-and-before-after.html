Event Detail Pages
Deep-dive on how two flagship events work
🎮 Social Event
Game Night
"Meet 3 new people. Win prizes. Have fun."
📅 Every Other Friday
🕕 6:00–8:30 PM
📍 Main Room
👤 Groups of 4
📋 How It Works
1
Arrive & get grouped
You're placed in a team of 4 strangers — randomized at the door
2
3 rounds of games
Tech trivia, mini-challenges, spaghetti + marshmallow build
3
Points & prizes
Top team wins. Peer-to-peer recognition each round
4
Free mix & mingle
Last 30 min — open floor, exchange contacts, connect on DojoConnect
🎯 Why This Works
  • ❌Removes the awkwardness of cold introductions
  • 🤝Forces teamwork before networking — builds real rapport
  • 🏆Healthy competition keeps energy high
  • 🔁People come back because it's fun, not just "useful"
  • 📲Warm handoff to DojoConnect — group chats form naturally
≥3
New people every member is guaranteed to meet per night — by design, not by chance
🚀 Showcase Event
Demo Day / Build Night
"Show what you're building. Get real feedback. Find collaborators."
📅 2nd Saturday / Month
🕑 1:00–4:00 PM
📍 Open Floor + Booths
👤 All members welcome
📋 Event Flow
1
Set up your booth
Any project in any state — laptop, prototype, sketch, idea
2
Walk-around hour
All attendees tour booths, ask questions, leave feedback cards
3
3-min lightning pitches
Optional: present to the room, get live Q&A
4
Community vote
Members vote for best project — winners get free month membership
🎯 Why This Works
  • 👀Shows members what others are building — sparks curiosity
  • 🤝Natural collaboration entry point — "I can help with that"
  • 🧠Feedback culture makes the space feel intellectually alive
  • 🏅Recognition makes people want to come back and improve
  • 🌱Surfaces hidden talent in the community
100%
Of attendees interact with at least one other member during the walk-around phase — interaction is built in, not optional
